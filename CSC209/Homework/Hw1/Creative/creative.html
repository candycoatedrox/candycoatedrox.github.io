The Structure of Slay the Princess

You're on a path in the woods, and at the end of that path is a cabin. And in the basement of that cabin is a Princess.

You're here to slay her. If you don't, it will be the end of the world.

Slay the Princess is a psychological horror visual novel made by Black Tabby Games. The game has a highly branching narrative, taking you through multiple "chapters" until you reach the end of a route and the cycle begins anew. In each new chapter, you gain a new Voice in your head, who will offer suggestions (and bicker with each other) alongside the Narrator.

  • You always begin in Chapter I: The Hero and the Princess with the Voice of the Hero.
  • Depending on your actions in Chapter I, you move on to a given Chapter II and gain another Voice.
    • Each Chapter II corresponds to one of the remaining 10 Voices.
  • With the exception of The Razor and The Stranger, all Chapter IIs follow a similar structure. Depending on your choices, you can either:
    • End the route in Chapter II;
    • Move on to that Chapter II's "unique" Chapter III, which is only reachable from this Chapter II;
    • Or move on to a "shared" Chapter III, which can also be reached from another Chapter II.
  • If you move on to Chapter III, you gain one of multiple possible Voices, depending on your specific actions in Chapter II.
